# Rock-height image: bounded material comparison

Target: recognizable small stone fractures on fixed mountain geometry, with the
same grass transition, palette, lighting, scenery and water. This prototype
replaces the two soft procedural face fields with one triplanar height image;
it does not add a second rock response. The512×512 image is generated artwork,
not measured physical height. Its source, bake and exact production ownership
remain review items during adoption.

## Controls and results

All views use1280×800, DPR1, fixed time0 and SwiftShader. The unchanged prototype
branch exactly reproduces its parent in all three shared-material fixtures.
Equivalent campaign/battle inputs produce identical bitmap pixels. Switching
on the image changes85,538 pixels near and9,588 far; the real-map views change
391,030 Alps /596,043 close Alps /197,293 Italy pixels. Cameras, source inputs,
geometry, vegetation and environment are unchanged within each pair. These are
lab routes over real geography, not the complete production campaign route.

All three bitmap regional views repeat with zero differing pixels and no page
or GPU-console errors. Comparison metrics and route stats are beside the images.
Source hashes freeze the reviewed prototype before adoption removes its controls.

## Visual judgment

Fresh unprimed comparison and direct inspection prefer the bitmap for exposed
rock detail: strongest in the close Alps, modest in the regional Alps and Italy.
Small cracks remain recognizable without obscuring the major ridges. The simple
ramp's far view has no clear winner because its fine fractures disappear there.

The bitmap still resembles cracks applied over a smooth face. Rounded landforms,
sawtooth grass boundaries and gray-olive stone remain; it does not establish the
reference's complete mountain character. This accepts a bounded material gain
for production adoption and consumer/lifetime verification, not final slice05.

The experiment switches and unused procedural alternative must disappear when
production adopts the shared resource. Source-band composition, real battle
terrain/vista, hardware timing and resource retirement are still unverified for
the adopted image. Existing canonical baselines remain unchanged here.
