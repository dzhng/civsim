## Decisions worth your audit

- **Expected values are closed forms, not recordings.** Probes sit where the fade collapses — the first cascade's unfaded near half (which exercises the zero-width-margin `select` guard, the NaN path), both overlap boundaries, the band's quarter points and midpoint, the second cascade's interior, `1 − 0.25k` on the terminal fade, and depths outside every interval. `cascadeBlendWeight` is checked against them but is labelled in the test as a **transcription of the same shader text** — it agrees, it does not corroborate. The independent claims are the collapsed forms plus three properties measured on hardware: per-cascade additivity, a covered receiver being fully shadowed, and the lit floor.
- **Tolerance fixed before hardware:** `2 × 1e-6 / m` where `m = 0.25·b²` (≈1.2e-4 at the shipped split), derived from the f32 dot-product error budget and the blend's division by the narrowest margin. A test pins that it is ~700× below the smallest gap between two distinct expected answers, so it cannot mask a wrong weight. Readback is `r32float` — no requantization.
- **The layer-swap symmetry trap.** My first configuration set was two exact mirrors, which cancels precisely at the break where both weights are ½ — a swap would have been invisible at the most important probe. Fixed by clearing one configuration **between** the two cascades' disjoint receiver-depth bands (0.62). A test now proves every probe any cascade weights moves under the swap; probes outside every interval deliberately do not, since no layer is read there.
- **Constant layers bypass PCF by construction** (all five taps identical), which is what makes the output the blend weight alone — and is also a stated limitation.

## Limitations — please read before trusting a green run

Synthetic cleared layers are not casters. This proves **nothing** about caster fitting, cascade motion under a moving camera, PCF softness, or whether a shadow reads correctly on screen. It is one numerical component gate for the receiver's layer binding, overlap blend and terminal fade.

Separately: I could not execute the GPU path, so a handful of WebGPU/TypeGPU runtime assumptions are unproven — the array-form `colorAttachments` with `targets: {format}`, `r32float` as a render target, and `probeBuffer.write(vec4f[])`. All three follow idioms already used elsewhere in this candidate, and any failure surfaces as a captured validation or page error rather than a silent pass.
