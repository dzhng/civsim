## Decision audit and next boundary

- **Unsound as a completion strategy, high confidence:** treating a deeper
  catch alone as sufficient. Lowering the hips under an almost stationary
  weapon improves a still silhouette, but does not automatically connect
  preparation, drive and braking. The next study must author that relationship
  across the whole sequence, not keep nudging the catch key.
- **Sound, high confidence:** preserving H's drive as an exact control for
  this experiment. It made the tested change attributable. It is not a rule
  that the next, broader sequence must preserve H's preparation or timing.
- **Provisional study choice, medium confidence:** fixed foot placements,
  grip landmarks and a stable shaft constrain a feasible experiment but do not
  prove weight transfer. Broader body motion must still satisfy those equipment
  and support controls, rather than moving hands or shield independently.
