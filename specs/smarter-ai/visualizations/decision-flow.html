Smarter campaign AI

Clone the state → imagine a few futures → score them → pick (with human-like randomness). Different factions weight the score differently. This page is the map; the slice files are the territory.

The hourly decision, rebuilt

Runs inside think() (ai.rs:137), once per 60 ticks per campaigning faction. Defend / recruit / build / merge stay as today's rules — only the offensive commitment goes through search.

1 · Candidates  plan::candidates()
a few labeled plans — focus, nearest-beatable, nearest-any, hold. Reuses today's target-finding; reads only what the faction can see (fog).
▼  for each candidate
2 · Imagine  state.clone() → rollout::forward(HORIZON)
apply the plan's marches, fast-forward ~120 ticks. Battles auto-resolve via resolve::estimate (cheap, O(units)). The clone means the live RNG stream is never touched → determinism holds.
▼
3 · Score  eval::score(weights)
one scalar: territory · army · income − threat. The weights are the faction's personality.
▼
4 · Choose  softmax(scores, temp, st.rng)
not pure argmax — sampled, so it sometimes takes the second-best line.
▼
5 · Commit  sim::try_move(...) — issue the chosen plan's orders for real.

Three places randomness enters (all from st.rng → still deterministic)

Perceived strength

Fuzz own/enemy strength while reasoning. Commits on the fuzzed number; reality resolves on the true one. → "miscalculated my army / felt brave."

Bravado / mood

A slowly-drifting per-faction scalar on the attack threshold. Streaky, not jittery — a faction runs hot or cautious for a while. Lives on the state (saved).

Selection temperature

Softmax over scores. Low = cold optimizer, high = erratic. Doubles as a persona dial.

Not random: the estimator

resolve::estimate stays pure so rollouts are repeatable. Battle uncertainty enters via perception, not the resolver.

Rivalry (slice 8) is not a persona. Any faction — turtle, merchant, warmonger — can carry a nemesis: seeded (Rome ↔ Carthage) or formed when attacked, escalating toward the strongest real threat and auto-dissolving once the power gap grows too wide. It's a thumb on the scale (prefer hurting / defending against the rival), not a separate brain, and it never overrides a clearly better opportunity elsewhere.

Lines we don't cross

  • Determinism — BTree-only, id order, RNG in state; rollouts clone before ticking. Pinned by a test.
  • Fog of war — search sees only st.visible, never omniscient.
  • The real battle is still the physics sim — estimate is the AI's imagination only.
  • No battle-physics changes — nothing under crates/sim.
  • Campaign-health harness (full_game.rs, lopsided_war_concludes) stays green.
