# Lower two-hand upright carry study

Candidate-only posture study. Archived A (`cbe3dc7a`) and B (`2634e2de`) controls
and evidence remain frozen. B improved clearance but its fresh A/B verdict was
equal, not naturalness acceptance. This study changes the carrying arrangement,
not merely the amplitude of its oscillation. No runtime appearance is promoted.

## Source decisions

The frozen pike extends 1.28 metres below its right-hand purchase. Lowering that
hand to the waist with a near-vertical shaft would ground the butt. The candidate
therefore keeps the lower-chest right purchase, eight centimetres below B, and
places the left purchase sixteen centimetres below it. Both hands remain on the
original shaft geometry. The right elbow is directed forward/down rather than
folding its forearm toward the opposite shoulder; the chest's static turn reduces
from 25 to 10 degrees. The shield-bearing left elbow hangs by the lower ribs.

A slight **rearward** upright rake (about 15.6 degrees from vertical) brings the
lower left purchase forward and aligns that wrist with its forearm. It also moves
the butt away from the advancing ankle. This is explicitly different from the
frozen original `pike-carry`, which points forward about 20 degrees above the
horizontal. The two appearance contexts are not relabeled or conflated: this is
ordinary held-hedge + atEase travel, not primary/forward-pike marching. Neither
pike length nor fitted geometry is changed to obtain clearance.

The upper-left/reversed-order study was rejected before a full film: the opposing
views showed a sharply cocked left wrist on a vertical forearm. A lower-left
forward-raked study then overfolded the left elbow and intersected the torso in
source probes. Those failures remain in scratch. The current rearward-raked
arrangement is a new candidate, not a reinterpretation of those failures.

## Controls and limits

All lower-body walk matrices and non-walk keys are preserved. The original rigid
carry's 0.1 mm palm-frame gate remains unchanged and does not pass this articulated
study (maximum 0.223 mm at quarter-frame samples). Actual finite-geometry purchase
is measured separately: nearest skin-vertex-to-shaft surface distance is at most
0.220 mm left and 0.163 mm right over 109 samples. Those are near-contact
measurements, not a claim based on the hand frame alone, and must accompany
opposing-view visual judgment.

The left wrist/forearm axis deviation is 0–7 degrees; right is 22–31 degrees.
Left elbow height spans 1.06–1.16 metres and right 1.20–1.28. Shield vertical
extent is 0.79–1.44 metres. Sampled butt clearance is at least 8.70 cm above the
floor; minimum butt-to-lower-leg surface distance is 13.55 cm, or 11.94 cm after
subtracting the spatial sampling-cell bound. These are sampled controls, not a
continuous collision proof. The inherited flat passing footfall and 2.27 mm sole
penetration remain outside this posture revision.

Re-authoring the candidate retains every non-walk GLB channel exactly; walk
channels differ only by at most 2.25e-7 from export float rounding. Export tangent
drift is handled only for this frozen-geometry motion comparison, after checking
all index/position/normal/UV/weight arrays exactly against the donor.
