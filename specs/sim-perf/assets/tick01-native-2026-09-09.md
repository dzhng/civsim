# Tick/01 — preserve contact snapshots, reject unproven caches

The shipped change reuses body owners and radii during iterative projection.
Those arrays are immutable between the initial body build and the impact
casualty bookkeeping after projection. Each pass still rebuilds body positions
and the spatial grid at exactly the original point. No new retained state ships.

## Bounded comparison and verdict

Two exact-state prototypes were measured independently: **grid** retained one
bucket ID per body, computed it once per rebuild, and reused the sorted layout
when memberships matched; **cache** added a bounded, direct-mapped ordered
query-bucket cache shared by targeting and projection. The query cache retained
first-occurrence bucket dedup and descending body order. Neither changed body
snapshots, candidate filters, owner dedup or strict-less-than target selection.

Every executable used the identical updated harness from `62157a8e`; the
original simulation was `38a018a1`. Runs were interleaved in the order below.
The early and developed columns are each the median of two repeat means, in
milliseconds per tick; idle is the full 600-tick mean. All runs are included.

| Run | Variant | Idle | Early 30k | Developed 30k |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | Original | 4.70 | 23.934 | 42.966 |
| 2 | Grid | 4.79 | 22.591 | 41.325 |
| 3 | Cache | 4.40 | 24.392 | 46.981 |
| 4 | Original | 4.50 | 26.301 | 55.654 |
| 5 | Cache | 4.42 | 23.047 | 38.326 |
| 6 | Grid | 4.47 | 23.076 | 39.104 |
| 7 | Original | 4.42 | 25.287 | 40.318 |

The original developed control moved from 42.966 to 55.654 to 40.318 ms.
Its individual repeats ranged from 39.816 to 62.800 ms. Task-owned builds and
tests were stopped, but unrelated browser activity remained; no unrelated
apps or processes were stopped. The grid prototype looked promising in the
early window, but its developed advantage was smaller than this control drift.
The query cache supplied no consistent additional benefit. **Both retained-state
mechanisms were removed**, rather than claiming a stable speedup from these
numbers or adding memory without a demonstrated developed-combat benefit.
The planned shared-neighborhood mechanism is therefore **rejected after a
measured trial, not delivered**. No additional tuning or favorable-run search
followed this finite sequence.

The metadata deletion is independently justified by the immutable inputs and
removes repeated writes without adding a replacement mechanism. This report
makes no numerical speedup claim for that final change. The integrating agent
owns final combined profiling and the standing budget verdict; these prototype
runs do not establish a portable budget deficit or justify changing mechanics
in response to host contention.

## Final metadata-only verification

Default `cargo check -p sim` and the golden state-hash test pass. The final
executable reproduces all-class duels at `0x8d21ca62c2a920c4` and seed-7 AI
through 9,000 ticks at `0xdda9a54e95963dbd`, exactly matching the original
oracles. Formatting is clean. No existing test or expected value changed,
and no cache-specific production code or test survives.

The shape review removed both unproven caches rather than preserving prototype
scaffolding. Diff review confirmed immutable owner/radius inputs, unchanged
position rebuilds, unchanged candidate traversal and unchanged casualty timing.
Documentation distinguishes the rejected plan from what shipped. An independent
read-only Codex review found no actionable defects and confirmed that this
report makes no speedup claim for the final metadata-only change. Full integrated
workspace, mechanics, scenario, balance and rebuilt-wasm visual gates remain
with the integrating agent.
