# Rejected direction-shuffle control

The shared wave baker orders wavelength and direction together. The shortest
five waves supply48.8% of slope energy; their principal/perpendicular variance
ratio is4.73. A seeded shuffle of only direction pairs preserved wavelengths,
amplitudes, phase ordering and global stratification while lowering that ratio
to1.63. Its focused numeric regression went red→green.

The rendered result was worse. Matched native production lake and actual
west-facing ocean captures show plaid-like lake crosshatching and long combed
sea streaks. Independent visual review preferred the control in both cases.
The shuffle and its new test were removed. Better isotropy alone does not prove
natural appearance, and this control must not be reintroduced from its test
result. No source spectrum change was accepted.

The sea control also reveals an independent defect: opaque, fully displaced
ocean triangles cross stationary field water in their24m overlap. Different
shore color inputs expose the angular intersection. A joined render boundary,
with zero displacement at the field edge, is the next pass; sim geography and
passability stay unchanged. The previous east-facing query hid this junction;
the shown control uses actual camYaw0 and zoom7.8.
