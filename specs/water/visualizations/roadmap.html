Replicate Spiri0/Threejs-WebGPU-IFFT-Ocean — in civsim's 2.5D renderer, Aegean palette. Bake off the technique, tune one variable per slice, integrate last.

The target

Deep choppy open sea, whitecaps everywhere, a single sun-glint streak. We replicate its wave geometry, foam, and glint behavior — but drive color from civsim's lighting preset (warm Aegean by day; dusk-capable). No dusk mood baked into the albedo.

Why this order. The look is judged on the open-sea surface rendered in the lab (the reference's actual subject, isolated from full-scene noise). Geometry → foam → glint are judged in neutral grey before color, so each verdict isolates one variable. Integration (S8–S10) only reconciles seams, depth, MSAA, and label/prop seating — it never re-litigates the look.
Biggest risks. No compute infra exists today (IFFT is net-new, lives behind the seam, falls back to Gerstner on weak GPUs). The camera bind group is VERTEX-visibility only — the clock change must move zero existing pixels. Campaign chart-vs-ocean tension is hard-gated to a subtle, zoom-aware treatment.
