# Independent visual review

Inspected all six full images, then cropped the projectile scene's forest and projectile field. No source/context inspection; candidate and baseline judged by visible content.

- **Forest readability is materially worse in the candidate (high confidence).** The overhead candidate forest is a flat, hard-edged olive footprint with long dark tree-shaped shadows and tiny speckled tree tops. The baseline has clear, solid crowns. The close camera candidate also reads as thin/bare trees versus solid baseline trees. This could reflect a different tree asset, but the candidate overhead image reads primarily as shadows without the objects casting them. Investigate foliage/LOD visibility before accepting. Crop evidence: `forest-actual-review.png`, `forest-base-review.png` alongside this report.
- **Soldiers and grounding are present (high confidence).** Candidate close view shows recognizable detailed soldiers, bows, shields and feet meeting the ground; rows have directional ground shadows. Overhead formations, flags and army positions remain visible and broadly correspond. No obvious whole formation loss. Candidate overhead colors sometimes have lower contrast against yellow grass than the dark baseline figures; this is a readability tradeoff, not established missing geometry.
- **Projectiles remain visible (high confidence presence, low confidence completeness).** Thin isolated dark strokes cross the lower-middle open field in both projectile images. Candidate crop `projectiles-actual-review.png` confirms them. Static frames cannot establish correct count, flight or impacts. Candidate first card is 105 versus baseline 104, so these frames are not identical simulation states.
- **Terrain has changed substantially, without clear geometry failure.** Candidate is warmer/yellower, with finer grass at close distance and a much harder brown circular terrain-patch edge. Baseline is muted green, with coarse grass and softer patch blending. Candidate camera-zoom field is more uniform whereas baseline is mottled. These are appearance differences; the hard circular patch is visually artificial but no missing ground or holes are visible.
- **HUD/minimap content remains functional-looking (high confidence).** Nine unit cards, counts, controls, army summary and minimap remain present. Candidate portraits are detailed and differentiated; baseline portraits are block models. Candidate adds a volume widget and total-man count, widening the army panel. No evident clipping or overlap. Minimap colored markers, forest/patch blobs and camera footprint are present in both.
- **Selection markers are not established by these images.** Flags and small blue/red faction markers are visible. No clearly selected unit is demonstrated, so these captures cannot verify selection outlines/rings or their depth ordering.

Verdict: substantial intentional-looking asset/style changes account for much of the diff, but the shadow-dominated forest is a concrete visual concern. The supplied images do not justify claiming forest rendering or selection behavior verified.
