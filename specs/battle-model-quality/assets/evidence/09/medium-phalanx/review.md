## Waist verdict and evidence

The [before front-hand sheet](before/front-hand.png) exposes a jagged pale wedge
between cuirass and belt. The old belt was a capped solid loft fitted to another
tunic. Trial 8 replaces it with a genuine leather wall and inner return on this
lining. Both suspension upper loops wrap the new band; lower sheath connections
and thickness vectors stay exact. No faces were excluded from the probes, no
threshold was relaxed and no global belt enlargement concealed the defect.

The matched [shield-hidden after](front/front-hand.png) and wider
[unobstructed waist](front/waist.png) show a continuous band with a narrow lining
seam. The first fresh reviewer preferred trial 8 moderately but withheld the exact
front verdict because the complete-kit shield obscured it. A second unprimed
front review preferred trial 8 with high confidence: the same exposed wedge is now
continuous. Parent inspection agreed. Label-excluded changed-pixel counts across
the matched tiles were front 9769, outer 611, rear 0 and front-quarter 5474. Those
counts locate change, not quality. The remaining thin seam is low-to-medium
severity close up and negligible in formation.

Earlier fresh reviews rejected tray-like shield orientation, unfinished waist
and open armor; the upright shield and actual-lining armor preceded this final
pass. Neither those reviews nor the seam verdict accept current hands, rigid
support tabs or the whole model.

The `after/` sheets are the actual full default scene output: whole soldier,
complete pike, armor, both hands, front/rear hands, waist and formation. `front/`
holds the matched shield-hidden supplement. `before/` is trial 7 material context.
