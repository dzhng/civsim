original near geometry (left) with the 2k prototype (right), at a fixed canonical
standing-height span of 32 physical pixels. Main admissions are asserted, all 36
captures repeat exactly while frozen, and each clip produces distinct frames.
Root reads every frame and an unprimed review finds no one-sided deformation,
pose timing, equipment or grounding difference. Candidate torso shading remains
lighter/speckled in crops. The shared subpixel spear appears dotted in both.

These sampled frozen phases and their GIF establish deformation readability at
this view, not real-time playback cadence or general acceptance. The separate
formation control rejects the 2k representation for its collective shading shift.

are not replacements for canonical software-rendered baselines.
