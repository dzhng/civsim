# Independent frame and shadow still-image review

Target: preserve readable soldier silhouettes and plausible front/back overlap over ground and sky in these small frame probes. Isolated shadow masks should preserve position, extent and edge falloff; mask agreement alone does not demonstrate correct lighting in a composed battle.

Inspected all 36 frame pairs (768×512) and four shadow pairs via sheets 0–6, with 2× crops of soldiers, horizon overlap, and local/whole-map shadow edges. Both sides have matching framing and subject layout; capture timing cannot be established from PNGs alone.

- **High confidence: no visible pair-level composition or depth regression.** Tactical probes show 12 soldiers with legible rank spacing; horizon view compresses the rear ranks behind the front four without obvious inverted overlap. Sky/ground boundaries and figure placements agree. Tiny pair differences do not establish which implementation is more correct. Full-frame RGB MAE spans 0.000192–0.002860 / 255, at most 1,029 changed pixels (0.262%). Larger per-pixel deltas (up to 58) are isolated, not broad scene changes. In the raw 1× tactical soldier crop (255,150)-(510,310), RGB MAE is 0.02408 with 786 changed pixels; horizon crop (300,200)-(475,280) has 19 changed pixels, MAE 0.00652. No corresponding missing geometry is apparent in the zooms.
- **High confidence: the first plain 1× frame is not repeat-identical.** Actual tactical→tactical-repeat changes 783 pixels for raw and TypeGPU, 814 for vgpu, with maximum channel delta 36. Bloom 1× and both 4× cases repeat exactly. This discrepancy is concentrated enough to be unobtrusive in these stills, but prevents calling every case deterministic. Temporal flicker or initialization cause cannot be diagnosed from two captures.
- **Medium confidence: contact grounding is weak in both frame candidates.** Soldiers stand against a uniformly green, softly textured plane with little visible foot-contact darkening. The flat plane ends in an angular finite boundary at the horizon. These are shared probe limitations rather than evidence of a port regression. Crops reveal normal pixel stepping at 1× and very thin weapons; no labels, markers, props, varied scenery or real terrain occluders are present to exercise their readability/order.
- **High confidence: isolated shadow shapes correspond.** Horizon masks are pixel-identical; local and local-repeat differ at seven pixels by one code value; whole-map differs at 45 pixels by one. Local edge crop contains all seven differences (MAE 0.000264); whole-map crop contains 43 (MAE 0.001033). Both show the same asymmetric softened right edge, and whole-map is substantially blurrier than local on both sides. Fine stipple/banding in the enlarged penumbra is shared. Neither side demonstrates a distinct edge defect or displaced mask. The mask subjects are elongated simple shapes; this does not verify contact with soldier feet or real scene shadow correctness.

Verdict: visually indistinguishable for these limited paired still probes, with a measurable 1× plain repeat-frame discrepancy to retain as an unresolved qualification. No performance, full-world completeness, animation smoothness, interactive resize, or composed shadow claim follows from these screenshots.
