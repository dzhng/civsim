## Walk-speed correction

The completed capture contains every frame from four bearings and all repeated
frozen crops matched. The two failures are ready/footwear pixel changes after
material correction, not page errors. It is fitting evidence only: inspection of
`crates/sim/src/movement.rs` shows `pace_speed` applies the class multiplier to
the above-walk range, leaving the 1.7m/s walking floor unchanged. The earlier
1.53m/s claim incorrectly multiplied that floor. The locomotion author is
correcting the walk and adding the 3.23m/s fresh heavy run without changing sim
behavior. Do not bless the old cadence as the actual gameplay walk.

Main and unprimed inspection covered all31 fitting frames in order, each with
four bearings. Both find rigid upper-body carry and shell-like cloth. The fresh
review additionally identifies the belt vanishing into the back garment during
the cycle and weak foot roll; ground sliding cannot be certified from these
fixed-position frames alone. The belt used one rigid spine weight while the
adjacent garment used transferred body weights. A local fitting correction now
uses the same body-weight transfer for the belt. The source and complete fitting frames are archived in
`combined-walk-fitting/`, including the old-speed looping GIF derivatives.
