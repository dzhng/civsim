Battle Experience Slice Roadmap

A five-step path from shared scenery ownership to terrain-readable battles, configurable quick battle, and durable shared-helper documentation.

01 shared

Scenery Model Sheets

Move reusable trees, rocks, carts, and generic props into shared review ownership before battle consumes them.

Gate: shared prop baselines under web/shots/models/shared/props.
02 contract

Terrain Feature Stream

Extract deterministic terrain features, edge roles, height fields, and full-field ground-cover styles.

Gate: pure feature tests plus edge, cover, and height assertions in a debug scene.
03 battle

3D Terrain Props

Render sealed side cliffs/ocean, fogged horizons, rolling ground, shared props, and grass/sand cover.

Gate: battle terrain screenshots inspected against the Aegean and edge-cliff references.
04 menu

Configurable Quick Battle

Choose maps, build both armies with 15,000 gold and 20 slots, or apply prebuilt templates.

Gate: menu flow scene launches and restarts the chosen setup.
05 docs

Docs and Height Audit

Document shared terrain/model ownership and classify every source-side flat-placement shortcut.

Gate: docs point to registries, and prop placement no longer hides behind z: 0.
