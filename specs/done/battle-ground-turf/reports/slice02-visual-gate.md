# Slice 02 visual gate

The final unprimed, ground-crop critique accepted the tuned contrast: broad,
low-amplitude olive/warm value drift retains tonal life without camouflage
islands or a discrete plate. The two far views preserve contrast amplitude and
scale without an in-world seam or scale pop.

The telemetry archive was corrected during slice 03 after finding that the
ground-only route hid blade geometry only once and the next production draw
made it visible again. With blade geometry persistently disabled before every
draw, mid-band RMS moves -81.63%, mean hue +1.56 degrees, and mean luminance
+0.54/255; hue and chroma spread both narrow. `assets/telemetry/contrast.json`
and the `corrected-*-rts.png` pair are authoritative; the old pair is retained
only as blade-confounded evidence.

An earlier unprimed review of the uncropped top-down frame rejected a soft
center-to-perimeter oval. Attribution showed that disabling the canopy makes
that oval substantially stronger, while roads, earth, rock, and scree share the
same perimeter wash. It is the fixed scene-wide fog/visibility envelope, not a
turf-only radial mask. The contrast-only slice therefore leaves it unchanged.

Non-gating observations remain assigned to later slices: dense procedural turf
dashes belong to slice 03; stair-stepped categorical material edges belong to
slice 04.
