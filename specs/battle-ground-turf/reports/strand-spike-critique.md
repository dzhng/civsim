# Strand spike visual verdict

Final verdict: **KILL**.

The first fresh critique found a viable technique but rejected the candidate's
uniform carpet weave, RTS near-field blur, and far-field scratch overlay. A second
controlled pass added broad density clustering, mixed lengths/curvature, continuous
global transforms, and gentler mip selection. A different fresh reviewer still
found the named contract red:

- top-down: fuzzy isotropic grain rather than tangled fibers;
- RTS near: repeated looping straw/noodle arcs over a flat base;
- RTS far: smooth but homogeneous minification with turf character lost;
- anti-tiling: no hard seam or lattice, but an obviously repeated loop vocabulary.

The sampler was mechanically deterministic and inside its hardware budget, so the
failure is visual technique suitability, not implementation instability. The next
production seam is anisotropic in-shader strand noise, not another canvas-bake tune.
