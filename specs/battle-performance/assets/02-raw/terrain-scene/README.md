The native owner requires an initial complete terrain input, retains the committed scene while replacements prepare, and publishes field/grid/cover/height/rectangle queries with the same resource generation. Existing pure source height, expanded-rectangle and zoom-style policy now have shared owners; no simulation or appearance policy changed.

A replacement constructs playable ground, horizon blockers or vista bands, ocean/lake water, scenery and the two underlays off to the side. Admission completes before swapping. A failed replacement destroys staging and preserves the previous scene; disposal cancels pending preparation and releases both generations. Scenery and backdrop initialization close error scopes before yielding, so concurrent rendering does not enter another owner's admission scopes. The scene exposes explicit opaque, transparent and shadow phases, with farFog separated from depth writers.
