# Fitted pike-family delivery

This pass completes functional roles before cosmetic refinement, following the
user's explicit roster-first quality decision. Engine equipment/posture remains
canonical; assets do not change movement, reach, attack timing or saves.

## Ownership and choices

- The saved fitted medium body owns pike geometry and purchases. Existing I
  thrust and eight earlier actions remain intact. The compatible heavy rig
  supplies body/leg reaction and protected-travel keys; medium connected arm
  chains replace sword-specific arms. No anatomy is rebuilt from the donor.
- Ordinary upright standing is a real new `at-ease` action. The old clip named
  `ready` is a diagonal one-hand pose, not an upright-rest substitute. The HTTP
  bundle/posed-mesh tracer fails when given that old clip (1.88046 m maximum
  height versus 1.84266 m held hedge). The corrected binding passes.
- Heavy pikes use fitted mail, shared crested-helmet equipment and a longer
  forward shaft beyond both purchases. Rest appearances reuse the corresponding
  physical primary source; their standing binding differs, not their anatomy.
  Sword sidearms contain actual sword equipment and compatible sword actions.
- The single family bake owns interpreted stride metadata and presentation.
  Primary medium retains its existing `medium/` bundle path;
  other family bundles use `soldier/`. Mid/far tiers are real reduced meshes,
  produced by the shared exporter, not repeated near geometry.
- Run calibration is the retained lower-body support measurement: 2.5146152 m
  over the authored .8 s, not the earlier 3.23 m/s comparison fixture target.
  Protected cycles retain their authored stride measurements. This is not a
  live ground-lock claim.
