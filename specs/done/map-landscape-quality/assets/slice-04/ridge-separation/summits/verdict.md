# Rejected summit field

Distinct jittered summit profiles remove the enclosing contour bands, but the resulting ranges lose their branching relief. The Alpine range becomes sparse smooth pyramids, the Italy chain loses subsidiary crests, and the close fixture becomes an extruded peak with broad uninterrupted faces. These regressions are visible in both clay and natural views. The previous field is less wrong.

This structural spike replaces contour-level ridges with the maximum of anisotropic summit slopes under the existing geographic envelope. It uses a bounded neighborhood with no persistent resource, graph or schema, but that architectural simplicity does not establish natural form. The losing code is removed. No material, lighting, water or vegetation parameter was changed.
