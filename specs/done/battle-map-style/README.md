# Battle Map Style — closed record (shipped 2026-07-05)

Every battle in the game is fought on a **procedurally generated map** in one
visual family: a rolling grass corridor walled east and west by 200–250 m
ranges, open north and south where the armies arrive, with lake pockets,
forest belts, and haze doing the reference's depth work. The three hand-built
maps are retired from the product surface (David's call, 2026-07-05); Quick
Battle offers three curated seeds — **Shore & Crags** (1), **Highland Vale**
(7), **Wooded Pass** (8) — plus a free Generated row that opens on a fresh
random seed with a live top-down preview. Campaign open-field battles boot
generated maps deterministically.

## Visual provenance — the standard the work was held to

- `assets/target-battle-map.png` — the painterly battle-map reference David
  supplied when the feature was commissioned. Drove the composition grammar:
  tall flank ranges, rolling grass, water pockets, haze layers.
- `.claude/skills/aesthetics/references/battle-overcast-highland.png` — the
  photographic register for the overcast-highland mood. The compose gate's
  style-family verdicts were judged against this image by unprimed agents.
- `assets/target-close-grass.png` — the ratified False Earth close-grass
  target. The close-gate oracle (`web/scenes/battle/battle-map-style.mjs`)
  still pins production grass against it pixel-statistically.
- `assets/topdown-heightmap-inspiration.png` — the heightmap the predecessor
  spec tried to replicate; kept as origin context (this spec deliberately
  generates the *family*, not that map).
- `assets/compose-round-1/` and `assets/compose-round-3/` — the compose
  gate's before/after evidence: round 1 was an honest FAIL (pancake plain,
  water dither, horizon band); round 3 was accepted for the overcast
  register. Reading the two side by side shows exactly what the fix lanes
  changed and why.
- `visualizations/seed-browser.{html,png}` — the 24-seed variety sheet,
  regenerated by the `battle-genmap-browser` scene; shows the full/dry/plain
  class spread and edge-seal grammar at a glance.

## Why it is shaped this way

- **The sim owns terrain truth; the renderer is a pure consumer.** The
  generator lives in `crates/sim/src/genmap/` and its output is certified in
  Rust (`crates/sim/tests/genmap.rs`); the photoreal renderer reads terrain
  through wasm pointers and a JSON descriptor. This is what makes generated
  battles replayable and the certificates meaningful — no visual layer can
  move a battlefield.
- **Total War tile-map model for the world beyond the fight.** One height
  function sampled at three bands: playable 4 m cells, a render-only vista
  apron at 16 m out to 2× the playable rect, and a far fog ring at 64 m out
  to ~3.5×. Chosen over a skybox/backdrop-card approach after researching how
  TW maps close their horizons; the invisible wall sits inside the E/W seal
  visuals so the boundary never reads as a wall.
- **E/W sealed, N/S open is a law, not a tendency.** Armies arrive north and
  south; the flanks are sealed by a seeded grammar (`genmap/edges.rs`:
  CliffRun / ForestBelt / WaterReach, weighted 6/2/2, organically shaped).
  Fog must saturate inside the far ring (the runway rule in
  `aerialPerspective.ts`) so the open ends dissolve rather than cut.
- **One owner per visual concept** — law, because every violation cost a
  debugging day: one grass architecture (the salvaged False Earth
  source-storage core in `bladeFieldLayer.ts`; ~25 rejected grass families
  are BANNED re-entries — see `specs/done/battle-map-reference/README.md`),
  one water material family (`seaLayer.ts` serves ocean, lakes, and field
  water), one mood owner (`CIVSIM_ENVIRONMENTS`), one aerial owner
  (`scene.fogNode` via `aerialPerspectiveNode`), one recipe-class owner
  (`genmap::recipe_class`, exported on the manifest — a duplicated JS
  derivation diverged once and is deleted).

## Invariants the code must keep honoring

- Same seed ⇒ byte-identical terrain (`generated_map_same_seed_is_byte_identical`;
  the seed-7 hash is pinned in cargo and in the scene suite).
- Every generated seed passes corridor + deployment certificates
  (`genmap/certify.rs`; 64-seed sweep with per-class assertions). Deployment
  bands (y ±600, x ±350) stay ≥95 % passable and slope-bounded in every
  recipe class.
- Recipe classes (full/dry/open-plain, 50/25/25) vary hydrology and interior
  relief only — seals, corridor, and deployment laws hold in all classes;
  plain maps keep a gentle swell above the billiard-table floor.
- Fog reaches full opacity inside the far fog ring on both axes and never
  saturates before the mid playable field (pinned in
  `photorealEnvironment.test.ts` in EFFECTIVE terms — the shader multiplies
  optical depth by `AERIAL_DISTANCE_SCALE`; unscaled world-km numbers lie).
- Fog may never launder geometry: the clay scene (`battle-genmap-clay`)
  re-judges landform with fog off.
- The north horizon is OPEN by design — the accepted composition pins the
  projected north perimeter at `HORIZON_TARGET` 0.187 of frame height. A 0.5
  "walling" target was considered and rejected; do not resurrect it.
- Curated seeds (1/7/8) are pinned by hash and class in
  `curated_generated_maps_are_pinned_and_certified`; a generator change moves
  them deliberately or not at all.
- Camera-travel resampling (the grass focus ring, any future streaming)
  scales with zoom and is throttled/async — David's renderer law, learned
  when an 8 m rebuild snap cost 25 fps on pan.

## Dead ends and hard-won lessons (do not re-walk)

- **Grass primitive families** — ~25 rejected attempts recorded in the
  predecessor spec's rejection ledger; only the False Earth source-storage
  core survived. Fix grass with parameters inside that architecture, never a
  new primitive.
- **Stamped geometry reads as fake**: axis-aligned forest/water seal
  rectangles (compose round 1), hard-threshold field-texture borders, and
  per-band N/S sink ramps (the "white horizon band" was a 7.5 m lit step
  where the far-fog ring restarted the vista band's ramp) all failed review;
  everything organic is jittered/tapered/continuous now.
- **Water that sparkles at distance is noise**: ripple-normal sun glint
  aliases into blue/white cobblestone by ~600 m; every water path fades its
  detail terms out by ~420 m. A blue-only water-pixel classifier scores real
  water 0 — water's honest signatures are specular-bright, blue, or overcast
  grey.
- **Turbidity is one axis with two jobs**: 9.8 satisfied the fog runway but
  erased the ranges the reference defines itself by; 7.2 lets ranges read
  through haze. The overcast dome ramp and the shadow-radius/test pins derive
  from the preset's turbidity — retune them together.
- **Wall-clock visual gates lie on software adapters**: animation cadence is
  asserted in sim ticks (15 ± 4.5 per march cycle = David's 500 ms spec);
  pixel checks gate on measured fps.
- **The compose gate works**: round 1 failed honestly (pancake plain — walls
  were 107–121 m and 700 m from the lens) and routed five defects to owners;
  round 3 was accepted after the vertical-presence rework. Two judge
  complaints were overruled *by the spec's own design* and recorded: the open
  north horizon, and golden-hour being judged against its own references
  rather than the overcast one.
- **Scene-writing traps** (each cost real time, now conventions): node-side
  constants/helpers referenced inside `page.evaluate` fail at runtime — pass
  them as arguments; JSON.stringify equality breaks on key order — compare
  fields; stats camera params can lie (`?camYaw` is the real rotation); wasm
  pointer reads detach on memory growth — scalars first, copy immediately;
  vite in git worktrees needs explicit `server.fs.allow` (a worktree's root
  `.git` is a file, so workspace detection fails); restart vite after wasm
  rebuilds (stale 404s).

## What diverged from the plan

- The horizon aspiration (0.5, "ranges wall the whole frame") died at the
  compose gate in favor of the open north (0.187) — see invariants.
- The vista was planned with a possible skybox/backdrop fork; research
  resolved it into the three-band tile-map model, and the legacy per-edge
  blocker meshes were deleted rather than evolved (generated maps report
  `sealedEdges: ["generated:vista"]`).
- Slice 19 (campaign seam) was marked cuttable but shipped; its seed
  derivation constrains to the certified pool rather than hashing freely, so
  every campaign battlefield is provably certificate-green.
- The never-rendered-sun-shadows regression (pre-dating this spec: the
  single-tier shadow fit parked the light *inside* its ortho volume) was
  found by this spec's re-bless sweeps and fixed in-flight, together with two
  stale photoreal scene framings that had been measuring giant near-camera
  grass blades instead of their subjects.
