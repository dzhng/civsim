# Rock normal control

The adopted bitmap's screen-derivative normal introduces conspicuous granular
interference on campaign slopes. Retain the image's fracture color and roughness
response, but use the terrain normal for dry-ground lighting in both consumers.
This removes a shader mechanism instead of adding another filtering policy.

The production campaign is frozen at tick0, natural terrain/no fog, camera
(-450,990), scale7.5, 1280×800 DPR1, bundled Chromium with SwiftShader flags.
The normal-detail control exactly matches the previous adopted production
capture (zero differing pixels). An in-page scratch intervention then replaces
only the terrain material normal node with its interpolated geometric normal;
581,752 pixels differ. No page errors occurred. This diagnostic overrides the
whole terrain normal node, including water: it establishes the rock-face cause,
not a water-preservation claim. The production correction instead retains the
existing water normal composition and needs its own captures.

[Full detail control](detail.png), [full geometric control](geometric.png),
[detail crop](detail-crop.png), [geometric crop](geometric-crop.png).
Crops are nearest-neighbor 2× views of x300,y150,width430,height400.

Independent unprimed inspection prefers the geometric treatment with high
confidence: full-frame lighting follows the slopes more coherently, while dark
cracks remain visible without the artificial granular pattern. Root agrees.
The tradeoff is softer, molded-clay-looking faces. Narrow ribs, sawtooth crests,
and absent intermediate ledges remain; this is not final mountain acceptance.
The experiment identifies the normal contribution, not the exact numerical
origin of its pixel-scale discontinuities.

A separate fresh review of these actual production frames prefers the image,
strongly close up and moderately regionally. It finds no new hard grass boundary,
but identifies broadly uniform crack coverage as cracked plaster over rounded
hills, particularly on Italy's gentler exposed ground. The material gain is
bounded; mountain form, grass-patch integration and bright steep-face roads stay
open. Root's inspection agrees. An earlier run was invalidated by development
reload after a scene-file edit and rejected by its camera guard; it is not part
