## Fixture simplicity versus visible errors

- **Isolated default/wide-detail fixtures:** empty surroundings, no scenery, and an exposed rectangular patch are compatible with deliberate isolation. These alone are not evidence of missing coverage or a rendering failure. Their sharp boundaries are visible, but not independently a defect in this presentation.
- **Horizon fixtures:** the patch-to-ground color/texture jump and finite ground silhouette are concrete visual discontinuities. They weaken the appearance of continuous terrain, even if deliberately exposed by a simple fixture. They do not demonstrate an implementation error or favor either side.
- **Limits:** still images cannot establish flicker or motion artifacts. The supplied horizon crops primarily inspect the sky, so close ground-artifact judgments rely on the full views. No obvious sky band edge or within-panel background seam is visible; subtle differences remain unresolved.
