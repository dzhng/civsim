# Independent visual critique

Target: soldiers should retain their silhouettes and placement, while directional shadows meet their feet and project consistently across the same ground plane. A repeated static view should not reverse its shadows. This target does not assume either filename is correct.

Inspected full tactical/horizon context plus enlarged on/off, actual/expected crops. No implementation or capture reports read; no browser/GPU work performed. All measured images are 768 × 512.

- **High confidence: initial tactical plain expected has a different shadow direction.** At both settings 1 and 4, expected casts leftward whereas actual casts rightward. This is obvious in full frames and 2× crops. Expected tactical-repeat plain then casts rightward and matches actual closely. Actual is less wrong on repeated-view consistency; absolute correct world-space light direction cannot be proved without a light reference.
- **High confidence: shadows are present and plausibly grounded in actual.** Rightward shadows begin near feet, lie along the ground plane, and shorten vertically under the horizon framing. Matched off images remove these ground shadows. No conspicuous floating shadow island or broad offset is visible. At 2×, soft shadows have fine repeating/dither-like texture; this occurs in matching expected images as well.
- **High confidence: no missing/extra soldiers or major terrain/sky composition difference between paired images.** Tactical shows 12 soldiers in a 4-column, 3-row arrangement; horizon obscures rear soldiers by perspective. Equipment silhouettes correspond. The rectangular terrain terminates against sky visibly at the horizon; it looks like a bounded test plane, not evidence of a complete world.
- **High confidence: repeat stability is conditional.** At setting 4 all actual tactical repeats are pixel-identical, with shadows on or off. All bloom repeats are identical for both settings/sides. At setting 1 actual plain repeat changes 788 RGB pixels on, 783 off, localized to soldiers; max channel delta 36, grayscale MAE 0.00212/0.00210. Expected shadow-on plain repeats change 16,250/16,933 pixels for setting 1/4 due chiefly to the direction change (MAE 0.33545/0.33590). Expected off plain repeats are identical.

Pair metrics: initial shadow-on tactical plain actual/expected grayscale MAE = 0.33748 (1), 0.33613 (4), with 17,148/17,164 changed RGB pixels. All other shadow-on pairs have grayscale MAE <= 0.000489; this includes tactical-repeat plain, all bloom, and horizon. At 4, horizon pair differences have max channel delta 1. Tiny residual pixel differences are not visible content loss.

Verdict: actual is less wrong than initial expected on static-repeat consistency. Other pairs are visually indistinguishable at the inspected scale. This supports these sparse static views and their supplied repeats only; it proves neither complete-scene coverage, moving-camera/animation temporal stability, nor performance.

Artifacts: metrics.json has every paired/repeated/on-off measurement. *-repeat-2x.png preserve aspect ratio at 2× and show full shadow extents; *-crops.png are labeled compact on/off montages (fit to panels, so use the 2× crops for geometry judgment).
