Civsim Debt Ledger

Every entry below names one concept that lacks a single owner, lists who owns it today, the home it would have if written today, what gets deleted in the same pass, and the consumer surface that proves the move. That is the refactor-clean shape: promote, collapse, verify through consumers.

Verdict

The codebase is clean at the line level and heavy at the shape level. Zero warnings, zero TODO markers, documented rationale on nearly every seam. The debt is almost entirely ownership: concepts that exist twice (sun direction, BFS, camera controls, spawn, bounding radius), god-modules that fuse five to twelve responsibilities, and a large estate kept alive solely as a lab exhibit.

Three findings dominate everything else by size. The lab-only bespoke battle passes, the renderer-lab router, and the two over-loaded scene and steering functions together account for roughly 17k lines that a reader must navigate but production never executes or never executes as one unit. Everything else is a day or less each.

The one decision gate

Retire the bespoke battle look estate, or keep paying for it. The archived renderer spec explicitly parked this as a human-feedback gate, not a fiat deletion. It is the single biggest lever in the repo and every other renderer item shrinks once it is decided.
