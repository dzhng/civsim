Neutral-albedo palette — base colors under neutral light (proposals to lock)

These are albedo under neutral daylight, not final lit pixels. The aesthetics reference shots are golden-hour, so their grass samples out dark/warm (~#858255); under the map's neutral light the same grass reads a touch fresher and lighter, so the chips lean that way. Warm "golden-hour" and cool "overcast-foggy" are environment lighting presets (Slice 06) applied over these neutral albedos — not amber/grey baked into the base color. Still vibrant in the near field, muted toward the horizon (aerial perspective, rule 1) — keep the punch up front.
