# Connected-cage hand — rejected study

Connected topology does not establish anatomical form. This local cage joined
the palm, finger webs and thumb into one subdivided surface, preserving the
existing wrist graft and donor weighting. It improved a root junction but
produced a more rectangular, featureless dorsal slab. Four repeated curved
bands still read as hoses, and the shorter lateral thumb opposed the fingers
less convincingly. Do not promote this construction or re-bless these shots.

The comparison target was a compact adult power grip, not agreement with an
earlier image. A is the settled, still-unaccepted [closure study](../power-grip-closure/review.md);
B is this folder's rejected candidate. A was less wrong anatomically. A fresh
unprimed reviewer inspected all six matched sheets, all 24 two-times crops,
whole-body context and the local Rome II hand reference. The main reviewer and
root independently agreed. Shield views 0 and 3 occlude the hand and provide no
hand-form validation. Whole-body context was the unchanged earlier study, not a
new whole-body capture of B.

## Why the contact gate was insufficient

The candidate was manifold, had zero sampled nonadjacent hand self-intersections,
and passed actual sword-grip, guard and shield-grip intersection checks over 930
poses. Nearest sampled finger gaps were approximately 1.09–1.23 mm. Those checks
establish neither a natural fist nor distributed grip support.

The handle axis remained 97 mm distal and 33 mm volar from the wrist, with a
17 mm radius. The distal palm control surface ended around 98–104 mm; the finger
root ring was at 112 mm. Measured central-volar-palm vertices had a median gap
of 14.5 mm to the actual handle; distal-volar vertices had a median gap of
5.06 mm. Nearest edge samples were much closer. These region-dependent vertex
statistics are not contact areas or anatomical reference measurements.

The handle is near the distal palm rim, encouraging a long exposed palm and
finger-dominated grasp. Its placement does not force planar metacarpal masses
or equal finger arcs: both are construction defects. A future study should
design the cupped palm, articulated MCP/PIP/DIP relationships and handle seating
together. A proximal handle shift is a hypothesis to test, not a validated
dimension or permission to change equipment silently. Preserve secure contact,
the non-hand body and rig while reconsidering this unaccepted anchor.
