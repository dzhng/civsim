an accepted manual candidate. The frozen `recipe.py` records its original authoring
context under `throwaway/heavy-death/`; its relative paths are not a runnable
entry point from this archive. The saved `.blend` is the editable checkpoint.

then inspected by the author, an independent critic and the integrating agent.

the shield/shoulder overlap, raised scabbard and pinched intermediate legs remain
unresolved. See `death-coarse.png` and `critique-final.txt`. No full film, continuous
contact, live blend, or final-art acceptance is claimed.

full-strength post-roll variants produce approximately -247.370, -26.179 and
-121.259 mm. `g-native-floor.json` preserves that diagnostic evidence. Earlier
