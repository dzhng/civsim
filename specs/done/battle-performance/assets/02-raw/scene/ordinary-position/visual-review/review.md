Reviewed all eight 2880×1800 source frames as full compositions, then the five paired crops alongside them. A is always left. No code/history, browser, GPU rendering, or intended-winner information used.

**Verdict:** no visible one-sided missing formation, changed formation footprint, displaced label, broken selection cue, or gross lighting/shadow defect identified. Neither version has an evident visual advantage in these stills. This is a bounded visual comparison, not acceptance from small image differences.

- **Tactical:** row spacing, unit silhouettes, blue/brown/bright material distinctions, long thin weapon marks, soft grounding shadows, and center arc/triangle/line cues persist on both sides. The native-size detail crop resolves mounted bodies and riders in both. The farther crop retains ordered ranks and bright repeated highlights, although individual equipment becomes difficult to identify. Some fine pixels differ, but no coherent missing piece or silhouette distortion emerges. Shared framing limitation: the closest row is cut by the bottom and side edges, preventing review of those complete formations.
- **Wide:** all 20 formation footprints remain visible in the same four-by-five arrangement. Relative color, density, spacing, center cue, and heavier dark shadow masses under some front formations remain consistent. Individual soldiers become repeated fine marks rather than readable characters. Labels are still separated, but the center label crosses the command cue and obscures part of it on both sides. Large empty sky/ground areas reduce the area available to inspect units.
- **Horizon:** both preserve small stippled rank blocks, some brown/blue/bright color differences, and fragments of the central cue. The four rows of labels overlap into a fence across the army and occlude much of the rear formations. Front formations are visible, but bodies, equipment, and individual shadows are below useful inspection scale. Yellow haze lowers contrast in both. The huge sky area and tiny army limit what this view can verify.
- **Far LOD:** visible front representations are compact colored rectangular swatches with a few bright rows/vertical shapes, rather than readable soldiers. Blue/brown accents and part of the red/orange cue survive on both. Fixed-size labels overlap horizontally and vertically, hiding most of the formation content; the rightmost text dominates while left labels are reduced to partial letters. Both share this severe readability defect. It is impossible to certify the concealed formation geometry or detailed shadows from this frame.

**Limits:** no movement, temporal smoothness, shimmer, animation, culling transitions, or LOD transition correctness can be inferred. Crops magnify existing pixels, not recover missing detail. Differences could include capture noise or real fine-detail rendering changes; the stills alone do not identify their cause. For the far views especially, label occlusion and tiny projected content prevent detailed equivalence claims.
