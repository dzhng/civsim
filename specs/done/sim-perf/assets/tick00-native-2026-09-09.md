isolated from that spread. Later changes need interleaved A/B diagnostics,
not a claim against one historical wall time. The standing gate remains two
repeats; additional diagnostics do not redefine it.

4.40/4.40 ms, then 4.38/4.39 ms, with identical hashes. That control found
