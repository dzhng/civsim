# 06 — Seed variety + seed browser

Prove seeds produce meaningfully different battlefields, and give David a
surface to sweep them.

## Contract unlocked

The generator is judged as a *generator* — variety, degeneracy rate, field
texture — not as one lucky seed. Quick battle gains pick + reroll.

## API seam

- Field-texture variety inside the corridor: passable forest clumps, scree
  patches, mud lowlands, roughness fields — all recipe-budgeted, all
  certificate-respecting.
- Wasm `generated_map_manifest(seed) -> String` (JSON: seed, groundCover,
  edges, feature summary). `mapCatalog.ts` gains a
  `generatedBattleMapEntry(manifest)` helper; the frozen 3-entry catalog is
  untouched.
- Quick-battle menu: a "Generated" row with seed input + reroll.

## Human can run

- The menu: pick, play, reroll.
- **Seed browser** (`specs/battle-map-style/visualizations/seed-browser.html`,
  regenerated by a scene run): ~24 seeds as clay + passability thumbnails in
  a grid — the human sweep for variety and degeneracy.

## Verification

- Cargo: certificate sweep widened (≥64 seeds); a coarse variety statistic
  (e.g. pairwise field-hash distance or feature-count spread) with a floor, so
  a collapsed generator fails loudly.
- Determinism: manifest and terrain agree for the same seed.
- Judged surface: the seed-browser sheet. Human checkpoint (non-blocking,
  preview-shots): "would you be happy getting these 24 battlefields?" Record
  verdicts per degenerate seed found; degeneracies become parameter fixes or
  new certificates, logged here.

## Stays green

Everything prior; menu flows for the 3 hand maps unchanged.

## Feedback that would change it

David flagging a *class* of degenerate seeds (corridor too canyon-like, lakes
always centered…) — each becomes a named certificate or budget, which is the
whole point of this slice existing before the style track composes.
