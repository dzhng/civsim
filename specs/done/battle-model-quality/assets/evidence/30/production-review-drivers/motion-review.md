The review films now consume the same published appearance manifests and manual
production poser as the static sheets. Source art, action bindings and renderer
behavior are unchanged. The adjacent frozen-catalog record identifies the inputs;
local copied hashed bundles are capture inputs, not this commit's deliverable.

The complete applicable set contains 104 phase sheets and matching GIFs across
twenty appearances, with 896 distinct captured poses per pass. The PNG samples
own the exact regression gate. GIF timing is quantized to centiseconds and depends
on the player; these derivatives are not live battle playback evidence. Looping
motions repeat, while death derivatives retain the final pose for review. All
GIFs decode at 360×360 with nonzero duration; the adjacent audit records their
actual frame counts and durations.

Direct review covered all chronological family bands and native sheets for the
small or ambiguous actions. Independent unprimed review covered every film and
sample, with sixteen native sheets inspected further. Both found complete visible
models and no frame-edge weapon/body clipping or catastrophic deformation. The
long-pike whole-kit framing necessarily limits body and grip detail.

## Retained limits

The fresh critique is retained verbatim. Small hit reactions, limited mounted
release silhouettes, short longsword extension, sharp sampled spear pose changes,
and coupled horse/rider collapse remain visible limitations. Sampled pose changes
do not establish an interpolation discontinuity. Ground contact and projected
body/equipment overlaps are not conclusively resolved by this camera.

The skirmisher release shows an empty throwing hand before the javelin returns
at recovery. This must be interpreted with the engine's release-at-entry event:
it is a post-release recovery/rearm clip, not evidence of a missing whole model
or an absent projectile event. Likewise the rigid artillery machine remains
present while its operator moves; this pass does not claim an animated firing
mechanism. These findings are recorded under the user's completion-first quality
decision, without another art refinement pass or a claim of flawless motion.

Three old shooting GIFs without an applicable current manifest role were removed,
so the artifact directory no longer presents those placeholder actions. Their
prior versions remain recoverable through Git. No production code or test
assertions changed in this artifact pass.

The existing driver remains the only capture and GIF owner; scratch overview
bands merely rearrange its PNG samples for inspection. Closeout shape and diff
review found no new mechanism, source change or copied catalog bundle to retain.
